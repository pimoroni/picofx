### Dual FX
[dual_fx.py](dual_fx.py)

Play a rainbow wave across MightyFX's RGB outputs and on to the RGB output of a TinyFX connected by I2C and running as an I2CTarget, with a pulse wave on the TinyFX's mono outputs.
