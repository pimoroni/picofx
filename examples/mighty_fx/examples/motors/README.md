# Mighty FX Micropython Motor Examples <!-- omit in toc -->

These are micropython examples for driving motors from a motor driver on one of Mighty FX's SP/CE connectors.

- [Examples](#examples)
  - [Motor Pair](#motor-pair)
  - [Motor Settings](#motor-settings)
  - [Motor Movements](#motor-movements)
  - [Motor Song](#motor-song)
  - [Remote Robot](#remote-robot)


## Examples

### Motor Pair
[motor_pair.py](motor_pair.py)

Sweep a pair of motors up and down their speed range together, on a SP/CE connector declared as a motor driver.

### Motor Settings
[motor_settings.py](motor_settings.py)

Show what a motor can be told beyond its speed, on a driver on SP/CE port A.

### Motor Movements
[motor_movements.py](motor_movements.py)

Drive a two wheeled robot through a fixed sequence of movements, on a driver on SP/CE port A.

### Motor Song
[motor_song.py](motor_song.py)

Play an evocative musical melody on MightyFX's motors, on a driver on SP/CE port A. Any resemblance to music you might have heard elsewhere is purely coincidental.

### Remote Robot
[remote_robot.py](remote_robot.py)

Drive a two wheeled robot from a Pimoroni Aye Arr Remote, its wheels turned by a motor driver on one of the SP/CE connectors.
