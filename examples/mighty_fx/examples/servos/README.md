# Mighty FX Micropython Servo Examples <!-- omit in toc -->

These are micropython examples for driving a servo from Mighty FX's L and R connectors.

- [Examples](#examples)
  - [Servo Sweep](#servo-sweep)
  - [Servo Pair](#servo-pair)
  - [Servo Easing](#servo-easing)
  - [Servo Calibration](#servo-calibration)
  - [Remote Robot](#remote-robot)


## Examples

### Servo Sweep
[servo_sweep.py](servo_sweep.py)

Sweep a servo from end to end on the L connector.

### Servo Pair
[servo_pair.py](servo_pair.py)

Sweep the servos on MightyFX's L and R connectors, together and then opposed.

### Servo Easing
[servo_easing.py](servo_easing.py)

Move a servo smoothly between random positions on MightyFX's L connector.

### Servo Calibration
[servo_calibration.py](servo_calibration.py)

Show the three kinds of servo MightyFX's L and R connectors can drive, and how to widen or rescale what their values mean.

### Remote Robot
[remote_robot.py](remote_robot.py)

Drive a two wheeled robot from a Pimoroni Aye Arr Remote, its wheels turned by a continuous rotation servo on each of the L and R connectors.
