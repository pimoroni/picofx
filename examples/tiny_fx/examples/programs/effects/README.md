## Mono Effect Examples

### Static Brightness
[mono/static_brightness.py](mono/static_brightness.py)

Show a static brightness on one of Tiny FX's outputs.

### Single Blink
[mono/single_blink.py](mono/single_blink.py)

Play a blinking effect on one of Tiny FX's outputs.

### Single Flashing
[mono/single_flashing.py](mono/single_flashing.py)

Play a flashing effect on one of Tiny FX's outputs.

### Single Flicker
[mono/single_flicker.py](mono/single_flicker.py)

Play a flickering effect on one of Tiny FX's outputs.

### Single Pulse
[mono/single_pulse.py](mono/single_pulse.py)

Play a pulsing effect on one of Tiny FX's outputs.

### Single Random
[mono/single_random.py](mono/single_random.py)

Play a randomly changing brightness effect on one of Tiny FX's outputs.

### Blink Wave
[mono/blink_wave.py](mono/blink_wave.py)

Play a wave of blinks on Tiny FX's outputs.

### Flashing Sequence
[mono/flashing_sequence.py](mono/flashing_sequence.py)

Play a flashing sequence across Tiny FX's outputs.

### Pulse Wave
[mono/pulse_wave.py](mono/pulse_wave.py)

Play a wave of pulses on Tiny FX's outputs.

### Binary Counter
[mono/binary_counter.py](mono/binary_counter.py)

Play an incrementing binary counter on Tiny FX's outputs.

### Traffic Light
[mono/traffic_light.py](mono/traffic_light.py)

Play a traffic light sequence on Tiny FX's outputs.

### Pelican Crossing
[mono/pelican_crossing.py](mono/pelican_crossing.py)

Play a pelican crossing sequence on five of Tiny FX's outputs, three for the traffic lights and two for the pedestrian figures.

### Sweep
[mono/sweep.py](mono/sweep.py)

Sweep a light back and forth across Tiny FX's outputs.

### Sweep Trail
[mono/sweep_trail.py](mono/sweep_trail.py)

Sweep a light back and forth across Tiny FX's outputs with a trail behind it.


## Colour Effect Examples

### Static RGB
[colour/static_rgb.py](colour/static_rgb.py)

Show a static colour on Tiny FX's RGB output.

### Static HSV
[colour/static_hsv.py](colour/static_hsv.py)

Show a static colour on Tiny FX's RGB output, using HSV.

### Blink
[colour/blink.py](colour/blink.py)

Play a blinking sequence effect on Tiny FX's RGB output. Each blink in the sequence can be a different colour.

### Rainbow
[colour/rainbow.py](colour/rainbow.py)

Play a rainbow effect on Tiny FX's RGB output.

### Random
[colour/random.py](colour/random.py)

Play a randomly changing brightness and colour effect on Tiny FX's RGB output.

### Hue Step
[colour/hue_step.py](colour/hue_step.py)

Play a stepped hue effect on Tiny FX's RGB output.
